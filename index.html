Cloze Reader

Learn the words from your books, in French.

Every word you looked up on your Kindle becomes a fill-in-the-blank card: the sentence from your book, a French definition as the clue, and the answer revealed in place.

  1. 1Drop your vocab.db
  2. 2Pick your books
  3. 3Download for Anki or Clozemaster

Your file is read in this browser and never uploaded.

Where do I find vocab.db?
  1. Connect your Kindle to your computer with a USB cable.
  2. Open the Kindle drive and go to system/vocabulary/. On a Mac, press ⌘⇧. if the system folder is hidden.
  3. Copy vocab.db to your computer, then drop it above.

        Why I made this

        I couldn’t find a tool built for full immersion. Most vocabulary tools lean on English translations, and few let you skip straight to activating the complex vocabulary you actually meet in books. Cloze Reader keeps everything in French, with your own sentences and French definitions as the clue, so words you once looked up become words you can recall and use.
